Website privacy · November 22, 2026

Your visit to this website

This notice describes the features of this website and the external services it links to.

Browsing the site

This site does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, a newsletter form, or a contact form. It does not use browser cookies or local storage for its own features. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, when it serves the site.

Donations

When online giving is available, donation buttons take you to a Stripe-hosted checkout. Payment information is entered on Stripe, not collected in this website’s pages. Stripe and the foundation receive information needed to process and administer the donation. See Stripe’s Privacy Policy for information about Stripe’s practices.

Social pages and contact

Instagram and Facebook links open the foundation’s official pages on those services. Their own privacy practices apply when you visit or contact the foundation there. This website does not embed their feeds or send a message on your behalf.

Questions

For questions about the foundation’s use of information you provide directly, contact Lola’s Butterfly Effect Foundation through Instagram or Facebook.
